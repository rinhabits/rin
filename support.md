# Rin support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email [rinhabits@gmail.com](mailto:rinhabits@gmail.com) and we'll get back to you.

**Your garden on a new phone.** Sign in to the same iCloud account and open Rin. Your goals come back on their own.

**Widgets.** Long-press your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Rin.
