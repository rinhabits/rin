# Privacy Policy — Rin

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Rin is a habit tracker designed to help you build routines without the pressure of streaks. Your goals and habits are yours. We've built Rin so we can't read them.

## Who we are

Rin is provided by Alexandra Denisova, an independent developer based in the Czech Republic, who is responsible for your data as described in this policy. Contact: [rinhabits@gmail.com](mailto:rinhabits@gmail.com)

## What we collect

Rin doesn't require an account, show advertisements, or track your activity across other apps and websites.

We use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/) for privacy-friendly analytics, to understand how Rin is used and improve it. These analytics are events such as finishing onboarding, checking off a habit, a goal reaching a new phase, or using a feature.

An event can include:

- A goal's day count and how many habits it has
- The goal's category, from Rin's own list
- Which feature was used
- The app version, iOS version, iPhone model and screen size, language and region settings, and display settings
- A hashed identifier that isn't linked to your name or Apple ID, and the time the event was received

We never send your goal names, habit names or anything else you type. We also don't send your time zone or the time on your phone.

TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. Analytics are used to understand how Rin works for people, never for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can learn more in [TelemetryDeck's privacy FAQ](https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/).

## Where your data lives

**On your device.** Your goals, habits, progress and settings are stored on your iPhone.

**In your iCloud.** When you're signed in to iCloud, Rin keeps a copy of your goals and habits in your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit, so your garden stays in sync across your devices. We don't run a server that stores your goals or habits, and we can't access your private iCloud database. Apple handles iCloud storage under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**In your widgets.** Rin's home screen widgets read the same data on your device to show your habits and flowers. Nothing from them is sent to us.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Rin schedules it on your device, along with a short recap of your week on Monday mornings. No outside notification service is used. You can turn notifications off in Rin's settings or in your iPhone's Settings.

## Your data and your choices

You can edit or delete your goals and habits in Rin.

You can export your history as a CSV file in Settings → Export CSV. The file is created on your device and only goes where you choose to share it.

Deleting Rin removes its data from that device. The copy in iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you delete it, in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. The exact steps can vary between iOS versions.

## Data retention

Your goals and habits stay on your device and in your iCloud until you remove them.

TelemetryDeck keeps anonymized analytics according to its own retention practices. These events aren't linked to an account, and we can't identify a single person's analytics history. See [TelemetryDeck's privacy FAQ](https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/).

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because your goals and habits live on your device and in your own iCloud, you can manage most of this directly in Rin and in Apple's settings.

For anything else, email us at the address below. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.
