# Privacy Policy — Rin

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Rin is a habit tracker designed to help you build routines without the pressure of streaks. Your goals and habits are yours. We've built Rin so we can't read them.

## Who we are

Rin is provided by Alexandra Denisova, an independent developer based in the Czech Republic, who is responsible for your data as described in this policy. Contact: [rinhabits@gmail.com](mailto:rinhabits@gmail.com)

## What we collect

Rin doesn't require an account, show advertisements, or track your activity across other apps and websites.

We use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/) for privacy-friendly analytics, to understand how Rin is used and improve it. These analytics are events such as finishing onboarding, checking off a habit, a goal reaching a new phase, or using a feature.

An event can include:

- A goal's day count and how many habits it has
- The goal's category, from Rin's own list
- Which feature was used, such as choosing a flower or linking a habit to Apple Health. For Apple Health, only the kind of data the habit is linked to (for example "steps") is recorded, never the data itself.
- The app version and build, iOS version, iPhone model and screen size, language and region settings, whether your phone is in light or dark mode, accessibility settings such as bold text, reduced motion and text size, and whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight
- A hashed identifier, derived from Apple's identifier for vendor, that tells one device from another without being linked to your name or Apple Account; a random session identifier that changes each time you come back to Rin; and the time the event was created

We never send your goal names, habit names or anything else you type. We also don't send your time zone or the time on your phone.

TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. Analytics are used to understand how Rin works for people, never for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can learn more in [TelemetryDeck's privacy FAQ](https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/).

## Where your data lives

**On your device.** Your goals, habits, progress and settings are stored on your iPhone.

**In your iCloud.** When you're signed in to iCloud, Rin keeps a copy of your goals and habits in your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit, so your garden stays in sync across your devices. We don't run a server that stores your goals or habits, and we can't access your private iCloud database. Apple handles iCloud storage under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**In your widgets.** Rin's home screen widgets read the same data on your device to show your habits and flowers. Nothing from them is sent to us.

## Apple Health

Rin can tick a habit for you when Apple Health shows you've done it, for example once you've walked 8,000 steps today. This is optional and off until you link a habit yourself.

When you first link a habit, Rin asks for permission to read four kinds of data: steps, exercise minutes, workouts and mindful minutes, so that habits you link later work without asking again. You can switch any of them off in that sheet. Rin only reads the kinds your linked habits use, and never asks to write anything to Apple Health.

- **What Rin reads:** today's total for the linked kind, on your iPhone, to check whether your target has been reached. For steps and exercise minutes it also reads the last seven days' total, for one purpose: a whole week of nothing means Apple Health isn't sharing that kind with Rin, and Rin can tell you instead of leaving the habit looking linked. The total is checked and discarded.
- **What Rin keeps:** only that the habit was ticked, the same as when you tick it yourself. Rin doesn't save your step counts, workouts or any other health readings, and doesn't put them in iCloud.
- **What Rin shares:** nothing. Health data never leaves your iPhone. It isn't sent to us or to TelemetryDeck, and it's never used for advertising or sold.

Apple Health may wake Rin in the background when new data arrives, so a habit can tick without you opening the app. The same rules apply.

You can unlink a habit any time from its goal's page, or from Edit goal → Tick from Apple Health, and turn off Rin's access in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Rin, or in your iPhone's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. If Apple Health is sharing nothing with Rin, Rin says so on the goal's page rather than leaving the habit looking linked.

## Purchases

Rin Pro can be bought as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a single payment. Buying is optional; the free version keeps working, and nothing you've already grown is taken away.

**Apple handles the payment, not us.** The purchase happens through the App Store, so your card details, billing address and Apple Account are never seen by Rin and never reach us. We can't charge you, refund you or cancel for you — that's all done through Apple, and you can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

What Rin does is ask the App Store whether this Apple Account currently has Pro. The answer is a yes or a no and which plan it is. We don't run a server for this and we keep no record of your purchase, beyond remembering on your iPhone that a purchase is waiting to be approved, so it can be counted once when it goes through.

**What goes to analytics:** that the Pro screen was shown and which screen it was opened from, that it was closed, that a purchase was started or completed and which of the three plans it was, that Restore was tapped and whether it found anything, and that a subscription ended. These are counts. No amounts, no card details, no Apple Account, nothing that identifies you.


## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Rin schedules it on your device, along with a short recap of your week on Monday mornings. You can also give a goal its own reminder, which names that goal's habits that are still open.

Notifications can name your goals and habits, and the evening reminder may lead with something Rin has noticed about your week, so this shows on your lock screen as any notification does. Rin can also tell you when a goal reaches a new phase while the app is closed: if Apple Health ticks a habit in the background, or a tick made in a widget arrives, Rin may find that the goal has moved on and say so.

All of this is worked out and scheduled on your iPhone. No outside notification service is used, and nothing in a notification is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in Rin's settings or in your iPhone's Settings.

## Your data and your choices

You can edit or delete your goals and habits in Rin.

You can export your history as a CSV file in Settings → Export CSV. The file is created on your device and only goes where you choose to share it.

Deleting Rin removes its data from that device. The copy in iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you delete it, in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. The exact steps can vary between iOS versions.

## Data retention

Your goals and habits stay on your device and in your iCloud until you remove them.

TelemetryDeck keeps anonymized analytics according to its own retention practices. These events aren't linked to an account, and we can't identify a single person's analytics history. See [TelemetryDeck's privacy FAQ](https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/).

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because your goals and habits live on your device and in your own iCloud, you can manage most of this directly in Rin and in Apple's settings.

For anything else, email us at the address below. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.
